About Us

From left to right: Jayden Decambre, Dongchan Kim, Hyunseok Hwang, Xiaofan (Sylvan) Shen, Vivian Xin Shu

SmartSight's team first came together during MIT Reality Hack 2026, where our prototype using Meta Ray-Ban AI glasses received the Grand Prize and the Meta Wearables Track Award.

Our team is interdisciplinary, bringing together expertise in AI systems, learning science, immersive technologies, and product design. Members include Stanford Computer Science and Harvard LDIT graduate alumni, with industry experience spanning AWS, Bloomberg, and AI/XR startups. This combination allows us to translate research insights into practical, buildable products.

Many of us personally experienced studying with limited feedback and support. SmartSight grew from that shared experience and a simple belief: wearable AI can deliver the kind of personalized guidance that once required expensive tutoring. Our mission is to build a scalable tool that expands access to effective learning support for everyone.
