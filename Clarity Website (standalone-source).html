How it works

From open notebook to review deck, automatically.

STEP 01
Wear your glasses. Study normally.
SmartSight runs in the background. Your glasses quietly capture notes, diagrams, and textbook pages as your eyes pass over them — no shutter, no app switching.
STEP 02
We read what you read.
An on-device model parses the material, identifies key concepts, and tracks which ones you've spent real time on versus which you've glossed over.
STEP 03
You hear what to do next.
When you pause, a quiet audio prompt tells you the single best next action — a flashcard to review, a concept to revisit, a short quiz to take.
STEP 04
Your progress shows up in the app.
Open the companion app when you want the big picture — session summaries, streaks, and behavior insights — all built from the material you actually covered.

About us

A small interdisciplinary team building wearable learning tools.

Our team came together during a wearable-AI hackathon in the spring of 2026, where our prototype — built around AI-powered smart glasses — won its track.

We bring together expertise in AI systems, learning science, immersive technologies, and product design, with alumni from top computer-science and education programs and prior work across cloud, finance, and XR research.

Many of us personally experienced studying with limited feedback and support. SmartSight grew from that shared experience and a simple belief: wearable AI can deliver the kind of personalized guidance that once required expensive tutoring.
