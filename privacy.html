Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

SmartSight is an AI study coach for iPhone that works with Meta Ray-Ban smart glasses or the phone camera. This policy explains what the SmartSight app and its servers collect, why, who processes it, and how you can delete it.

What we collect

  • Account details. Your email address and, if you provide it, your name. You sign in with Apple, Google, or an email and password. Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash.
  • Text from your study pages. During a study session your glasses or phone camera take photos of what you are studying. The app reads the words in each photo on your phone, using Apple's on-device text recognition, and sends us only that text. The photos themselves are not uploaded.
  • Study activity. Sessions and their timing, the topics detected in the text from your pages, bookmarks and notes, quizzes and flashcards with your answers, and your chats with the coach.
  • Study profile. Information you choose to give the coach, such as your exam, exam date, target score and weekly study hours.
  • Voice. When you talk to the voice coach, Luna, your microphone audio is streamed to Google's Gemini Live service for a real-time reply. SmartSight's servers do not record or store this audio. Wake-word detection (“Hey Luna”) uses Apple's speech recognition, on the device where supported.
  • On your phone only. Your study photos stay on your phone. The app removes them after about seven days and keeps at most 500, except photos for items you bookmark, which stay until you remove the bookmark. All of them are removed when you delete the app.
  • Photos from earlier versions. Before the app read pages on the phone, bookmarks could include a photo stored on our servers. If you bookmarked items with an earlier version, those photos remain there until you delete the bookmark or your account.

SmartSight does not show ads, does not use advertising trackers, and does not sell your data.

How we use it

  • To sort the text from your pages by subject and topic and track time spent on each.
  • To generate quizzes, flashcards, session summaries and coaching for you.
  • To keep you signed in and protect the service from abuse.

Who processes it

  • Supabase stores the database, and any bookmark photos saved by earlier versions of the app.
  • Render hosts the SmartSight API servers.
  • Google (Gemini) sorts the text from your pages by topic and runs the voice coach. Google and OpenAI generate quizzes, flashcards, summaries and coach replies from your study activity. Both process this data under their API terms.
  • Apple and Google handle sign-in when you choose them.
  • Meta's Wearables SDK connects the app to your glasses. Photos taken through the glasses go from the glasses to the app on your phone and stay there. The SDK may also send Meta information about the connection between your glasses and the app, under the Meta Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Profile → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and all associated study data from our servers, including any bookmark photos saved by earlier versions of the app. Photos on your phone are removed when you delete the app. Signing out or deleting the app does not delete your account. You can also email us to request deletion.

Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, so please avoid capturing pages with text you would not want stored, such as passwords or other people's private information.

Children

SmartSight is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Beta testing

If you test SmartSight through Apple's TestFlight, Apple also shares crash reports and the feedback you submit with us, under Apple's own privacy policy.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: hello@getsmartsight.com
