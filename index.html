Live coaching

Ask Luna about the page you're on

Say "Hey Luna" mid-session. She already knows what's on your page, so "explain this" just works.

Talking with Luna

  1. "Hey Luna, explain this."
  2. "That's the chain rule: differentiate the outside, then multiply by the inside's derivative. Want an example?"
  3. "Quiz me on this page."
  4. "Okay. What's the derivative of sin(x²)?"
  5. "Highlight this."
  6. "Got it, highlighted."

Luna

Luna, the coach who knows what you studied

Talk or type. Luna knows what you've studied, so "What should I do today?" gets a real answer.

Daily plan

A plan for today, built from your sessions

A few things to do today, each with a reason: what's fading, what you left unfinished, what's due.

Knowledge Vault

Knowledge Vault: everything you've read, sorted

Every page is filed by subject and topic. Open one to see how much you've covered.

Quizzes and flashcards

Quizzes and flashcards made from your pages

Exam-style questions from what you actually studied. Flashcards come back when they're due.

We're five people from AI systems, learning science, immersive tech and product design: Stanford Computer Science and Harvard LDIT alumni, with time at AWS, Bloomberg and AI and XR startups.
