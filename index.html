A coach is only as good as what it can see.

Most study tools only see what you type into them, so their advice is based on a fraction of your work.

SmartSight logs what you study on its own: every page, every topic, every hour, and how you score afterwards. With the whole picture, it can tell you what to work on next, and why.

Live coaching

Ask Luna about the page you're on

Mid-session, just say "Hey Luna." She already knows what's on your page, so "explain this" or "quiz me on this" needs no setup, and "highlight this" saves the page for later. Then she goes quiet again.

  • Hands-free, through your glasses or your phone
  • Knows the text on the page you're reading
  • Speaks only when you ask

Talking with Luna

  1. "Hey Luna, explain this."
  2. "That's the chain rule: differentiate the outside, then multiply by the inside's derivative. Want an example?"
  3. "Quiz me on this page."
  4. "Okay. What's the derivative of sin(x²)?"
  5. "Highlight this."
  6. "Got it, highlighted."

Luna

Luna, the coach who knows what you studied

Say "Hey Luna" or type in the app. Luna knows what you've studied and how you've scored, so "What should I do today?" gets a specific answer, and "Quiz me" starts one on the spot.

  • Voice or text
  • Answers come with cards: your plan, your week, what you know
  • Quizzes, flashcards and step-by-step explanations inside the chat

Daily plan

A plan for today, written from your own results

SmartSight picks the next few things to do and says why: the topic you score lowest on, the question you missed, the cards that are due. Finish them and it shows what moved.

  • Countdown to your exam date
  • A note on how your last plan went
  • Streaks, levels and achievements that track real work

Knowledge Vault

Knowledge Vault: everything you've read, sorted

Pages are filed under the right subject and topic as you study them. Open any topic to see how much of it you've covered, how long you've spent, and what to practise.

  • Progress for every subject, with weak ones flagged as Focus
  • Bookmark a passage and it's saved with its topic

Quizzes and flashcards

Quizzes and flashcards made from your pages

Questions are written from the material you actually studied, in exam style, with an explanation after every answer. Flashcards come back when they're due, including the ones you got wrong.
